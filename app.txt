App Name: FocusForge
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: Gamified Productivity
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free + $4.99/month Pro

Description:
A deep-work timer app where every completed focused work block generates building materials to construct, decorate, and expand a small isometric digital village.

Key Features:
- Pomodoro-style focus timer (custom durations 10-90 min)
- Each completed block earns resources: wood, stone, gold
- Build and place isometric buildings in your village
- Village grows as you maintain focus streaks
- Session log showing daily/weekly focus hours
- Background ambient sounds (forest, rain, cafe)
- Statistics: total focus hours, most productive day/hour
- Focus goals and project tagging

Target Audience: Knowledge workers, students, freelancers, developers
Competitors: Forest app, Focusmate, Be Focused
Unique Value: The village-building reward creates deeper motivation than simple tree-growing
